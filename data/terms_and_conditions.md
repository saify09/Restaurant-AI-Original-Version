# Terms and Conditions

1. Refund Eligibility
- Refunds are only eligible if requested within 10 minutes of order placement.
- No refunds for orders already in "PREPARING" or "COMPLETED" status.
- Technical failures in the app are eligible for full refund regardless of time.

2. Cancellation Policy
- Orders can be cancelled for free within 5 minutes.
- After 5 minutes, a 50% cancellation fee applies if the order is not yet "PREPARING".
- Once "PREPARING", cancellation is not allowed.

3. Pricing
- Prices are subject to change without notice.
- If a price mismatch occurs between menu and checkout, the lower price will be honored.

4. Data Usage
- User data is used strictly for order fulfillment and platform improvement.
