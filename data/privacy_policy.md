# Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

2. How We Use Data
- To process orders and provide customer support.
- To improve our AI agent's performance through anonymized reasoning logs.

3. Third Parties
- We do not share data with third parties except for payment processing (simulated).

4. Safety
- All AI decisions are logged for auditability and risk management.
